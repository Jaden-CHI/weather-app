## Google Play

### Short description (80자 이내)
```
Weather forecasts for your golf rounds in Korea — tee-time alerts & smart tips.
```

### Full description
```
Golf Windy is the weather app built for golfers playing in South Korea.

Planning a round? Golf Windy watches the forecast for your tee time and tells
you — in plain language — whether to play, prepare, or cancel.

◉ ROUND-DAY WEATHER, NOT CITY WEATHER
Forecasts are pinned to the golf course itself, focused on your tee-off window
(1 hour before to 5 hours after), so you see the weather that actually matters
for your round.

◉ SMART ROUND SUITABILITY
A simple GREEN / YELLOW / RED verdict for every scheduled round, based on rain
probability, wind speed, temperature and lightning. Set your personal
sensitivity to rain, wind, cold and heat — the verdict adapts to how YOU play.

◉ CANCELLATION GUIDANCE
Most Korean golf courses allow free cancellation until a deadline. Golf Windy
counts down your cancellation window and warns you before it closes when bad
weather is coming.

◉ CALENDAR IMPORT
One tap imports golf rounds from your phone calendar. Courses are matched to
our database automatically — and courses we don't know yet are found via
portal search, complete with address and location.

◉ SCORECARD OCR
Snap a photo of your paper scorecard and Golf Windy reads the scores — track
your rounds and companions over time.

◉ HOME & LOCK SCREEN WIDGETS
Your next round's D-day, weather and verdict, always one glance away.

◉ MORE
• Wind-focused course map
• Restaurant suggestions near the course
• Round weather notifications, on your schedule
• Korean and English, following your device language

Golf Windy currently covers golf courses in South Korea — perfect for
residents, expats and traveling golfers teeing up in Korea.

No sign-up. No personal data collected.
```

## 스크린샷 플랜 (영어 로케일로 촬영)

촬영 규격
- iOS: 6.9" (iPhone 17 Pro Max, 1320×2868) 필수 — 6.5"는 6.9"에서 자동 축소 지원
- Android: 휴대전화 1080×2400 이상, 최소 2장 (권장 8장)

| # | 화면 | 캡션(영문) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 홈 (D-day 카드 + GREEN 판정) | Know before you go — round-day weather at a glance |
| 2 | 라운드 적합도 RED/YELLOW 상태 | Smart verdicts: play, prepare, or cancel |
| 3 | 설정 > 라운드 성향 | Tune it to how you play — rain, wind, cold, heat |
| 4 | 일정 + 캘린더 가져오기 | Import rounds from your calendar in one tap |
| 5 | 시간별 예보 상세 | Hour-by-hour forecast for your tee-off window |
| 6 | 지도 (바람) | Wind-focused course map |
| 7 | 스코어카드 OCR | Snap your scorecard — scores read automatically |
| 8 | 홈 위젯 | Your next round, always one glance away |

촬영 방법: 시뮬레이터(영어 설정) → 앱 언어 자동 전환 확인 → 캡처.
캡션 오버레이·프레임은 기존 한국어 스크린샷 템플릿과 동일 스타일 권장.

## 출시 체크리스트 (영어권 공개)

1. Play Console / App Store Connect에 **영어(en-US) 현지화 추가** 후 위 텍스트 입력
2. 스토어 **국가/지역 배포 범위 확장** (현재 한국만이라면 영어권 국가 추가)
3. iOS: App Store Connect > App Information > Localizations에 English 추가
4. 스크린샷 8장(영어 UI) 업로드
5. 개인정보처리방침 영문 페이지 준비 (양 스토어 링크 요구)
